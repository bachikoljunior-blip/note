- Correct arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24792

### Verified claim
The UAI paper develops online FDR procedures using online e-closure and online compound e-values, explicitly targeting continuously generated hypothesis streams and retaining FDR control under arbitrary dependence among the testing evidence. It reports strict power improvements over prior e-/p-value procedures and an `O(log t)` rejection-decision algorithm.

### Scope guard for archive/QD use
This is **not** direct evidence that an adaptive QD archive can simply attach an e-value to every newly generated candidate and inherit the theorem. “Arbitrary dependence” does not mean “arbitrary invalid evidence after adaptive selection.” A QD application still needs a valid test/e-value construction for the null attached to each adaptively generated candidate and a formally compatible online protocol. Until that mapping is proved, e-closure remains a candidate error-budget mechanism, not a certified archive controller.

### Decision-relevant consequence
For repeated archive admissions, the right research question is not merely “which multiple-testing correction?” but “can the candidate-generation/evaluation interface produce valid sequential evidence after adaptive selection?” Any controller proposal must make this evidence-validity boundary explicit.

## C46 — QD archive coverage is an audit structure, not automatic search/repair superiority

### Primary source
Ibne Farabi Shihab, Fariya Afrin, **“Quality-Diversity Stress Tests for Process Reward Models: What Archive Coverage Can and Cannot Certify,”** arXiv:2608.08008v1, submitted 2026-08-08.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08008

### Mechanism
The paper uses MAP-Elites to organize correctness-flipping PRM attacks into a behavior-indexed archive, while separating visitation/exploit coverage from robustness certification. Its strongest useful lesson for population controllers is a negative one: archive structure can improve auditability without proving that QD is the best search algorithm or the best repair-data source.

### Quantitative evidence
On the reported small 25-cell attack grid:
- With the same **45 evaluations per problem**, MAP-Elites obtains exploit-cell coverage `0.40`; random perturbation also `0.40`; exhaustive enumeration `0.48`; single-objective search `0.18`.
- The paper explicitly notes that the budget exceeds the 25-cell grid and exhaustive enumeration is the strongest per-problem searcher at this scale.
- On the larger Qwen2.5-Math-PRM-7B discovery audit, 107 verified base traces are attacked (allocated 5,400 evaluations/readout; 4,815 actually consumed after exact-match filtering). Mean pooling yields **44 strict exploits**, exploit-cell coverage `0.40`, and maximum gain `0.294`; minimum pooling yields **1 strict exploit** for this tested mechanism.
- In paired repair v2 on 54 evaluation problems, the pre-repair strict exploit rate is `0.148 (8/54)`. QD repair seeds reduce it to `0.037`, `0.056`, and `0.074`; random and exhaustive repair-source controls each reach `0.037 (2/54)` in their reported seeds. The authors therefore attribute the repair gain primarily to adversarial fine-tuning, not a superiority of QD-selected repair examples.

### Tested-scope interpretation
This does **not** show QD is broadly inferior. The descriptor grid is small, discrete, and cheaply enumerable; the authors themselves limit the negative search conclusion to this scale. QD remains useful here as a behavior-indexed audit object and may gain relative value when the behavior space is large, continuous, or not directly enumerable.

### Population-controller implication
Before allocating a population/island/QD layer, test a matched-budget direct/random enumeration baseline. A QD controller should earn its complexity by improving one of:
1. discovery under non-enumerable/large descriptor spaces,
2. coverage of qualitatively distinct failure modes,
3. preservation of useful stepping stones,
4. audit/replay structure,
rather than by assuming that diversity machinery itself improves endpoint quality.

This strengthens the standing “effective independent information per unit cost” principle: **use QD when the search geometry warrants it, not merely because a population is available.**
