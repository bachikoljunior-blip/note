## C45 formal mapping tightened — what online e-closure actually requires

Primary source:
Ziyu Xu, Lasse Fischer, Aaditya Ramdas, **“Improving online FDR procedures via online analogs of e-closure and compound e-values,”** UAI 2026, PMLR 337:7552–7561; arXiv:2603.24792v3.
- https://proceedings.mlr.press/v337/xu26a.html
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24792

The previous checkpoint corrected the paper identifier. This pass inspected the actual formal definitions.

### Verified formal interface
The paper defines the observed-information filtration for e-values as
`F_t = sigma(E_1,...,E_t)`.
Its online e-closure construction requires candidate rejection collections at time `t` to be measurable with respect to `F_t`. For the e-collection construction, intersection e-values `E_S` must themselves be valid e-values for the intersection null and be measurable by `F_sup(S)`.

For donation-based online compound e-values, with a fixed nonnegative weight sequence `gamma` summing to at most one, validity is defined through a weighted true-null expectation budget:
`sum_{i in N} gamma_i E[tilde(E_i)] <= 1`.
The paper permits arbitrary dependence among the original e-values and even permits the donation sequence to be arbitrarily dependent on them, but this is downstream of the requirement that the original evidence stream is valid e-value evidence for its null hypotheses.

### Minimal archive-admission mapping
A plausible QD mapping would be:
- hypothesis `H_t`: candidate `x_t` does **not** improve the true target-cell objective over the incumbent by a predeclared margin while satisfying the required constraints;
- evidence `E_t`: a valid e-value against `H_t` from a fresh evaluation protocol;
- rejection of `H_t`: permission to treat `x_t` as a statistically supported improvement candidate;
- archive mutation: a separate systems action that may additionally require safety/descriptor/reproducibility checks.

But the UAI theorem does not by itself prove that `E_t` remains a valid e-value when `x_t` is adaptively generated from the complete prior archive/evaluation history. The paper's “arbitrary dependence” guarantee is about dependence among valid evidence variables, not a license to construct invalid evidence after outcome-dependent candidate selection.

### Required condition before adoption
For an archive controller, one of the following must be established:
1. fresh evaluation data whose conditional law makes the selected candidate's e-value valid despite adaptive proposal selection;
2. a selective/sequential e-process explicitly proved valid under that candidate-selection mechanism; or
3. a compound construction whose true-null expectation budget is proved for the adaptive candidate stream.

Without this, e-closure is useful theory but not an archive-level error certificate.

### Additional limitation
Even valid FDR control over **admission decisions** does not automatically control final-archive regret, lineage corruption, or descendant propagation after a false admission. A population controller needs a loss mapping from false discoveries to archive dynamics; otherwise “FDR <= alpha” and “archive remains reliable” are different claims.

## C47 — noisy elitism directly favors lucky archive entries; adaptive re-evaluation needs a reservoir

Primary source:
Niels Justesen, Sebastian Risi, Jean-Baptiste Mouret, **“MAP-Elites for Noisy Domains by Adaptive Sampling,”** GECCO 2019 Companion, DOI 10.1145/3319619.3321904.
https://njustesen.github.io/njustesen/publications/justesen2019map.pdf

### Mechanism
The paper identifies a specific failure of elitist MAP-Elites under noisy fitness/behavior descriptors: repeated one-shot comparisons cause the archive to overestimate fitness and preferentially retain unstable “lucky” solutions. It proposes:
- adaptive sampling: challenger and incumbent receive additional evaluations only as needed for the comparison;
- drifting elites: re-estimated behavior descriptors can move an elite between cells;
- a depth-`k` reservoir in each cell so moving/rejected elites do not leave the cell empty and previously nonelite candidates can become active.

### Quantitative / ablation evidence
The short paper reports:
- using cell depth `k=10` improved results relative to `k=1`;
- increasing to `k=100` produced no observed further difference, an early diminishing-return signal for backup-population depth;
- on noisy-performance Rastrigin, the adaptive method gives the best corrected total normalized quality among explicit averaging baselines `n=1,10,100`;
- with both noisy performance and noisy descriptors, fixed-sample archives degrade over time as unstable solutions populate the archive;
- on stochastic BipedalWalker, fixed `n=10` performs best and the adaptive method follows it, showing the adaptive rule is not uniformly optimal.

### Tested scope / uncertainty
This is a two-page GECCO Companion study. Rastrigin experiments were repeated only three times and BipedalWalker once; it is not evidence for a universal reservoir depth or a universal adaptive-sampling rule. Noise is Gaussian in the synthetic tasks and environmental in BipedalWalker, not LLM-judge false-admit/false-reject noise.

### Population-controller implication
A one-shot “challenger score > incumbent score => permanent replace” rule is brittle under stochastic evaluation. A more robust controller should:
- keep a small reversible backup reservoir / Standby set per niche;
- spend extra evaluation on incumbent-vs-challenger disputes rather than uniformly on all candidates;
- allow incumbents to lose status after re-evaluation;
- avoid very deep backup populations unless measured value justifies them.

This aligns with the later Active/Standby/Terminated evidence already in the clean chain, but C47 supplies a QD-specific archive mechanism rather than a multi-agent messaging mechanism.

## Controller synthesis update

The evidence now separates three layers that should not be collapsed:

1. **Proposal diversity:** number/niches/lineages of candidate generators.
2. **Comparison reliability:** adaptive re-evaluation or sequential evidence for challenger-vs-incumbent decisions.
3. **Archive reversibility:** backup/reservoir/Standby state so an erroneous admission or eviction is recoverable.

Increasing layer 1 cannot compensate for weak layers 2–3; it may amplify the number of irreversible wrong archive updates.

A practical default hypothesis for future matched-budget experiments is therefore:
- start with a small niche population plus a small per-niche reservoir;
- allocate additional verifier calls to contested replacements;
- make promotion/eviction reversible until deeper evidence is available;
- treat global error-budget theory as optional only after per-candidate evidence validity is established.
