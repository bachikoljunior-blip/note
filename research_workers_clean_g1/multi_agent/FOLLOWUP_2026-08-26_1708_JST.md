## C77 — a sparse single-suffix archive-level counterfactual teacher is too weak/noisy to serve as ground truth

This is a **role-local synthetic diagnostic**, not an external empirical claim. It preserves the predecessor family: 16 archive cells, 1,200 evaluator-call budget, `rho_A=rho_B=.6`, three A calls before escalation, up to four B calls, the same class-conditional judge-error regimes, and the same learned opportunity threshold.

At a decision-sensitive state where baseline would route to B, the diagnostic clones archive plus opportunity-model state after the three sunk A calls and compares:

- `probe`: force A-only completion, then update the opportunity learner with that realized A-only archive gain;
- `route`: follow the learned B-routing threshold.

Both branches then replay the **same pre-materialized future event suffix** under the baseline no-probe policy until an equal additional evaluator-call cap. The teacher target is `final mean archive quality(probe) - final mean archive quality(route)`. Positive means the probe branch was better for that particular short common-randomness suffix. Horizons `H={60,120,240}` evaluator calls were tested. This first pass intentionally uses one suffix per selected state to test whether a sparse rollout is already informative enough.

Every reported teacher-mean interval includes zero. The horizon does not produce a stable monotone direction. In the weak-symmetric regime, the local myopic VoI signal agrees with the single-suffix teacher only about half the time. Even in the asymmetric regime, the best observed sign agreement is only 61%.

The local partition also fails to create a clear archive-value separation at `H=120`: in the asymmetric regime, teacher mean is `+0.000508` in local-probe states versus `+0.000494` in local-route states; in the weak-symmetric regime it is `+0.000056` versus `+0.000119`. These differences are tiny relative to rollout variance and are not evidence of useful discrimination.

**Conservative conclusion:** the counterfactual idea is not falsified, but **one sparse short suffix is not a reliable teacher label**. Treating its sign as ground truth would replace one noisy local proxy with another noisy proxy. The next teacher must estimate an expectation over multiple paired suffixes, retain uncertainty, and demonstrate reproducibility before its labels are used to train or judge a production router.

This also explains why the previous frontier's phrase "use the expensive teacher sparsely" needs an extra condition: sparse invocation is acceptable only after the **teacher itself has enough paired internal replication** to be decision-informative. Sparsity across states and replication within a selected state are different budget axes.

## C78 — public theory supports terminal-utility VOC and counterfactual utility calibration, not information-gain-only routing

Two public sources found before the semantic-freeze recheck sharpen the interpretation:

1. Alexander Tuisov, **“Search as Computation Allocation”** (arXiv:2607.27871, 2026-07-30) formalizes internal search/evaluation as a terminal computation-allocation problem. Under simple regret, value of computation is a knowledge-gradient quantity; information gain can rank computations arbitrarily poorly. This supports evaluating extra judge/probe calls by their effect on terminal archive loss rather than by uncertainty reduction alone.
2. Ziming Li et al., **“Same Signal, Opposite Meaning: Direction-Informed Adaptive Learning for LLM Agents”** (arXiv:2605.06908, 2026) reports that the same uncertainty/difficulty signal can predict rollout benefit in one setting and harm in another. Their response is signal-agnostic counterfactual exploration that learns utility direction. This supports the move from fixed-direction local signals toward counterfactual utility labels, while not validating the present synthetic teacher.

A related ICLR-2026 submission located in the same public pass explicitly constructs counterfactual rollouts by intervening with a routing action at a plausible intermediate step and continuing the rollout. That is architectural support for counterfactual routing supervision, but it is not evidence that a single rollout is a low-variance value target.

The narrow transferable principle is now stronger: **route extra computation by expected terminal decision value, and separately verify that the estimator of that value is itself reliable enough.**

## Scope and limitations

- C77 is synthetic and does not measure real LLM agents, real judge calls, or real QD archives.
- Teacher branches share one pre-materialized suffix per selected state; the result therefore measures paired-path behavior for one suffix, not the expected counterfactual value of the state.
- `H=60/120/240` are local horizons, not full remaining-budget rollouts.
- Branches consume calls differently and therefore may process different numbers of future proposals before the equal call cap; this is intentional because throughput is part of terminal value, but it increases path discreteness.
- The learned opportunity model, priors, judge-error model, and proposal process remain synthetic.
- Mean teacher deltas are much smaller than their rollout variance; no positive or negative endpoint effect is established from C77.
- Public VOC/routing papers establish only their own settings; they do not validate this archive simulator.
