## Main update: external receipt evidence, capture epochs, and rollback proof strength separate cleanly

This run followed the prior `CaptureScopeContract` frontier with a deterministic synthetic study that injects:
- self-reported middle/tail omission, duplicate and reorder;
- receiver-side receipt suppression;
- transparency-log suppression and equivocation;
- dynamic surface registration bypass;
- topology split/merge without capture-epoch rollover;
- incomplete runtime dependency capture plus conservative static complement.

It keeps five proof axes separate:
1. `artifact_valid`;
2. `presented_chain_complete`;
3. `capture_scope_complete`;
4. `dependency_edges_complete`;
5. `descendant_closure_complete`.

All numbers below are **synthetic mechanism-study results**, not production thresholds.

### 1. Evidence layers catch different omission classes

On 50,000 deterministic runs, treating any injected omission/capture/epoch defect as the target event:

| evidence mode | precision | recall | false-positive rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| self-reported chain + local close | 1.0000 | 0.4708 | 0.0000 |
| + receiver-attested receipts | 1.0000 | 0.5782 | 0.0000 |
| + transparency publication/checkpoint | 1.0000 | 0.6713 | 0.0000 |
| + coarse unmatched-activity sentinel | 0.9493 | 0.9978 | 0.0255 |

Interpretation within this model:
- receiver attestation adds real residual coverage over a self-written chain;
- transparency adds further coverage, especially for receiver/log suppression and equivocation;
- neither cryptographic chain integrity nor receiver/transparency evidence alone proves that an action used an uninstrumented surface or that a topology mutation kept the capture manifest valid;
- a coarse external sentinel can nearly close that detection gap here, but only by accepting false positives. It is a detector, not a completeness proof.

This matches the normative limitation in the SCITT AI-Agent Action Receipt draft: a receipt attests the signed record, but explicitly does **not** attest capture completeness, truth/completeness/timeliness of inputs, or that no action occurred outside the instrumented boundary. The same section says equivocation is not detected merely by verifying one presented branch; transparency registration also needs consistency monitoring.

Primary source: `https://ftp.otenet.gr/doc/internet-drafts/draft-noa-scitt-ai-agent-receipt-01.html`

HANSARD independently makes the same boundary explicit: five out-of-agent choke points can make omissions on mediated channels detectable, while effects through unwitnessed channels leave no discontinuity, so unwitnessed-channel enumeration is prerequisite to a completeness claim.

Primary source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22512`

| positive scope + full dependency-edge proof | 1.0000 | 1.0000 |
| positive scope + descendant-closure proof | 1.0000 | 1.0000 |
| whole redraw | 1.0000 | 1.0000 |

The important negative result is the stale-epoch case: an otherwise clean-looking manifest that ignores the topology digest admits unsafe local replay in about one third of those synthetic runs. Requiring a current capture epoch turns the stale topology into a fail-closed fallback instead.

This is consistent with Autonomous Topology Mutation, which gates structural changes with capability monotonicity, state-routing completeness and shadow-before-live validation rather than letting the prior topology's assumptions silently survive a split/merge.

Primary source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20488`

### 3. Capture-scope completeness is still weaker than rollback admissibility

Across all 50,000 runs:

| proof axis | fraction true in the synthetic generator |
|---|---:|
| artifact valid | 0.9413 |
| presented chain complete | 0.8916 |
| capture scope complete | 0.6814 |
| all dependency edges complete | 0.0693 |
| corrected-source descendant closure complete | 0.8221 |

The low full-edge-completeness rate is useful: requiring **every dependency edge in the whole graph** is much stronger than necessary for one source-local repair. The target property is the corrected source's descendant closure, not global graph perfection.

Matched rollback results:

| policy | recovery | mean replay nodes | correct endpoints / 100k replay-node cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| observed-only local | 0.5581 | 8.2250 | 6,785 |
| warning-only | 0.8942 | 17.5043 | 5,108 |
| epoch-blind manifest | 0.8865 | 16.8317 | 5,267 |
| positive capture scope only | 0.8914 | 17.0723 | 5,221 |
| positive scope + **all dependency edges complete** | 1.0000 | 29.8638 | 3,349 |
| positive scope + **descendant closure complete** | **1.0000** | **19.3231** | **5,175** |
| whole redraw | 1.0000 | 31.0000 | 3,226 |

Within this scoped model, a truthful positive descendant-closure proof is much less conservative than proving the entire graph complete while retaining the same fail-closed recovery. This sharpens the prior contract design: the proof target should be **repair-specific closure admissibility**, not global provenance perfection.

DART provides independent support for that distinction from a different recovery formulation: checkpoint legality alone is not semantic recoverability; the runtime needs explicit admissibility conditions and must block local rollback when no admissible checkpoint exists. In its commitment-sensitive evaluated cases, entry-only restore fails while certified admissible checkpoints succeed with much smaller replay than whole-task rerun.

Primary source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23311`

### 4. Candidate `CaptureScopeContract` refinement

The contract should now carry two separate epoch-scoped proof families:

```text
capture_epoch
  epoch_id
  topology_digest
  surface_inventory_digest
  mediated_channel_set_digest
  known_unmediated_channels[]
  runtime_tool_registry_digest
  wrapper_registry_digest
  sealed_before_live

receipt_evidence
  self_chain_head
  receiver_receipt_head
  transparency_checkpoint
  consistency_monitor_state
  unmatched_activity_sentinel_state

proof_axes
  artifact_valid
  presented_chain_complete
  capture_scope_complete
  dependency_edges_complete            # global / optional
  descendant_closure_complete(source_version)

rollback_admissibility(source_version)
  capture_epoch_current
  correction_scope_identified
  descendant_closure_complete
  committed_effect_constraints_satisfied
  else: fail_closed_to_enlarged_scope_or_whole_redraw
```

A topology digest change must invalidate the old capture epoch until a new scope is sealed before live traffic. A valid receipt chain cannot substitute for capture-scope proof, and capture-scope proof cannot substitute for repair-specific descendant-closure proof.

## Scope / negative evidence

- The DAGs, probabilities, sentinel operating point and replay-node cost are synthetic.
- Receiver/transparency precision of 1.0 is a property of this injected-fault model, not a real-world claim.
- The combined sentinel's 99.8% recall is not a completeness guarantee; it explicitly trades false positives for detection.
- `descendant_closure_complete=true` is a truthful synthetic oracle-like contract in this experiment. Current frameworks have not been shown here to generate that proof reliably.
- Full dependency-edge completeness is intentionally stronger than necessary for the tested source-local rollback and should not be taken as the required production contract.
- SCITT draft-01 is an Internet-Draft; HANSARD, DART and ATM are research papers/preprints with their own tested scopes.
