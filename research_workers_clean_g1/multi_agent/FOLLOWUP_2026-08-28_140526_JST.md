## New aggregate result: disagreement predicts the need for a novel adjudicated diagnosis

By initial disagreement pattern:

- all three distinct: new final tuple in `35/112 = 31.25%` (`23.23%–40.23%`).

The increase across disagreement strata is not just a small descriptive fluctuation in this finite corpus: chi-square independence gives `chi2=20.05`, `df=2`, `p=4.43e-05`; a Cochran–Armitage ordinal trend test gives `z=4.458`, two-sided `p=8.28e-06`. This does **not** prove that disagreement causally creates better adjudications, nor that the final human adjudication is infallible. It does show that a controller which only enlarges a union over already-proposed diagnoses becomes increasingly incomplete precisely when the proposal set is more internally discordant.
