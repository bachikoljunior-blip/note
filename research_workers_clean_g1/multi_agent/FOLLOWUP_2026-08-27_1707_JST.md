## Positive capture-scope contract changes the problem

A second study isolates the value of a **positive, out-of-band capture-scope contract**. Here all declared/listed dependency surfaces are modeled as completely represented in the normal union graph. Only `custom_wrapper` / `reflection` can become jointly blind.

Three regimes are separated:
1. `silent_union`: no external knowledge that a blind surface was used;
2. `manifest_awareness`: an external scope registry knows an unrepresented surface was exercised and fails closed to whole redraw;
3. `mediated_capture`: an ideal chokepoint mediator records the actual dependency edge even when the ordinary runtime/static mechanisms are blind.

At joint blind probability 0.40:

| policy | full-closure recovery | mean replay nodes | correct endpoints / 100k replay-call units |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| silent union | 68.60% | 6.04 | 9,745 |
| perfect manifest awareness + fail-closed redraw | **100%** | 14.30 | 6,534 |
| ideal mediated capture | **100%** | 7.00 | **12,506** |
| 90%-sensitive manifest detector | 96.98% | 13.49 | 6,692 |
| whole redraw | 100% | 29.00 | 3,333 |

This cleanly separates three claims that should not be conflated:
- a capture-scope **manifest** can turn a silent unknown into an explicit fail-closed decision;
- a manifest alone does not provide the missing descendant edges and therefore may need broad redraw;
- actual **mediation at the dependency chokepoint** can preserve both completeness and local-replay efficiency in this idealized model.

The 90%-sensitive detector is also important negative evidence: even a strong but incomplete scope detector leaves residual stale-state risk. `positive evidence` needs an explicit guarantee/scope; it should not be inferred from a high empirical detection rate.

## New public evidence

Two public papers reinforce the architectural distinction without validating these synthetic percentages:

- **Autonomous Topology Mutation (ATM)**, arXiv:2607.20488, submitted 2026-06-04, gates runtime structural mutations on capability monotonicity, **state-routing completeness**, and shadow-before-live validation. Its state-routing invariant requires every memory atom to be routed to a permitted child or explicitly dropped, and it uses a shadow window before committing a structural change. On its reported 720 deterministic-tool task runs, its factorisation ablation raises code-task success from 3.3% to 61.7%. The relevant transferable idea is not that number, but that structural mutation needs a positive state-routing invariant rather than silence-based confidence. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20488
- **Runtime Skill Audit**, arXiv:2606.11671, submitted 2026-06-10, shows why static appearance is insufficient when behavior is runtime-conditional. On 100 skills it reports 90% accuracy, 88% TPR, and 8% FPR, 13 points above its best static baseline; under its self-evolving attack setup, static detectors collapse after one or two rounds while runtime probing continues detecting 19–20/20 malicious skills. This is a security audit rather than dependency rollback evidence, but it independently supports adding runtime mediation/probing for surfaces whose behavior emerges only under execution. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11671

## Updated controller hypothesis

For destructive correction + selective replay, a useful proof object should distinguish at least:

1. **surface inventory completeness** — are all state-carrying channels that this execution may use positively enumerated?
2. **mediation completeness** — does each used channel cross an instrumented chokepoint that can emit a versioned dependency receipt?
3. **closure completeness** — do the emitted receipts plus any conservative static complement contain all descendants of the corrected version?
4. **overcapture/blast radius** — how much benign independent work is added when the proof is conservative?

Candidate routing rule:
- positive surface + mediation + closure proof -> local/union replay;
- positive surface inventory but missing edge mediation -> fail closed or use an independently complete complement;
- `known_uncaptured` -> expand scope;
- silent/unknown/unlisted surface without a positive manifest -> do **not** interpret absence of evidence as local-replay eligibility;
- structural topology mutation should additionally use shadow-before-live validation because the blast radius extends beyond one correction.

## Scope guards / negative evidence

- All rollback/recovery percentages above are deterministic synthetic mechanism results, not production thresholds.
- `mediated_capture` is an idealized positive contract; it assumes the chokepoint itself is complete and honest.
- A manifest that merely lists a surface does not prove descendant-edge capture.
- High marginal runtime/static recall does not imply low joint miss probability.
- Runtime Skill Audit is security auditing, not rollback evaluation; its accuracy numbers cannot be transferred to provenance capture.
- ATM's state-routing completeness concerns memory routing during topology mutation, not arbitrary dependency-closure repair; it supports the invariant shape, not this experiment's quantitative outcomes.
- The replay-call metric is a mechanism proxy, not wall-clock, token, or dollar cost.
