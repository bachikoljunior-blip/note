## Main update: integrity proof, capture-scope proof, and descendant-closure proof are three different things

This run followed the prior frontier and converted the earlier synthetic `positive-proof` flag into a more explicit proof stack:

1. **artifact / chain integrity** — the receipts presented are internally well-linked, non-duplicated and ordered;
2. **capture-scope completeness** — every action/dependency surface that is allowed to become live must pass a declared mediated boundary during the current capture epoch;
3. **dependency / descendant-closure completeness** — for the corrected datum, every state/result that actually consumed the old version is included in the replay closure.

The key result is negative: proving (1) or even (1)+(2) is not enough to safely perform local destructive repair. Local rollback only became fail-closed in the synthetic model when (3) was positively established as well.

### Public-source cross-checks

**SCITT AI-Agent Action Receipts draft-01 (15 Aug 2026)** makes the same separation explicit at the evidence layer:
- a chain verifier detects broken links, duplicate sequence numbers, sequence gaps and scope mismatches in the presented chain;
- the chain must be presented from genesis;
- a checkpoint can expose truncation relative to that authenticated checkpoint;
- but a prover can withhold both a tail and the checkpoint that would reveal it;
- most importantly, the profile explicitly says it provides **no capture-completeness** and does not prove no action occurred outside the instrumented boundary.

Source:
`https://ftp.otenet.gr/doc/internet-drafts/draft-noa-scitt-ai-agent-receipt-01.html`

**Notarized Agents / Sello (arXiv:2606.04193)** moves action evidence to the receiver side and a transparency log, directly addressing self-reported trace omission. Its stated residual limitations include the **suppression attack**, service collusion and adoption incentives. This reinforces that stronger receipt integrity still does not automatically establish complete capture of every relevant action.

Source:
`https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04193`

**Autonomous Topology Mutation (arXiv:2607.20488)** provides a useful structural analogue for capture epochs. It gates runtime topology changes with capability monotonicity, state-routing completeness and shadow-before-live validation. In particular, every state atom must be routed to an allowed child or explicitly dropped, and the candidate topology cannot receive live traffic before shadow validation. This supports treating split/merge as an epoch-changing event whose state/capture contract must be re-established before live execution, rather than letting a prior capture manifest silently survive a topology mutation.

Source:
`https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20488`

## Experiment A: weak / non-complete capture boundary

In the weak environment, dynamic surfaces may be declared, known-unmediated or silently absent from inventory. A true capture-scope completeness proof is therefore unavailable by construction.

30,000 deterministic DAGs:

| policy | recovery | mean replay cost | correct endpoints / 100k replay-cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| absence-as-safe | 0.334467 | 5.8077 | 5,759 |
| warning-only | 0.960000 | 26.9186 | 3,566 |
| empirical runtime∪static union | 0.696367 | 9.1245 | 7,632 |
| manifest/no-warning pseudo-proof | 0.908933 | 22.1417 | 4,105 |
| capture-scope-proof-only | 1.000000 | 29.0000 | 3,448 |
| descendant-closure-proof-only | 1.000000 | 29.0000 | 3,448 |
| conservative enlargement | 0.999867 | 28.6636 | 3,488 |
| whole redraw | 1.000000 | 29.0000 | 3,448 |

Interpretation:
- the apparent `capture-scope-proof-only` safety comes from **refusing to issue that proof at all** in this weak deployment; it therefore degenerates to whole redraw;
- treating “no warning” as proof is materially unsafe: about 9.1% of runs retain stale descendants;
- the empirical union has the best throughput-like proxy but only 69.6% full recovery, showing why safety and efficiency must remain separate objectives;
- a high average throughput metric can hide stale-state acceptance.

## Experiment B: mandatory mediated surface registry

The second environment forces every dynamic surface creation through a registration/capture boundary and requires a close checkpoint before a capture-scope proof can be issued. This eliminates silent surface-inventory bypass in the model, but **does not make every dependency edge complete**.

30,000 deterministic DAGs:

| policy | recovery | mean replay cost | correct endpoints / 100k replay-cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| absence-as-safe | 0.642233 | 7.7966 | 8,237 |
| warning-only | 0.738933 | 12.3312 | 5,992 |
| empirical runtime∪static union | 0.843567 | 9.6616 | 8,731 |
| manifest/no-warning pseudo-proof | 0.882367 | 13.7257 | 6,429 |
| **capture-scope-proof-only** | **0.900400** | 15.9538 | 5,644 |
| **descendant-closure-proof-only** | **1.000000** | **26.6179** | **3,757** |
| conservative enlargement | 0.999933 | 26.2000 | 3,817 |
| whole redraw | 1.000000 | 29.0000 | 3,448 |

The decisive comparison is:
- capture-scope completeness alone: **90.04%** recovery;
- descendant-closure completeness proof: **100%** recovery;
- whole redraw: **100%** recovery.

In this scoped model, a truthful closure proof preserved the fail-closed recovery of whole redraw while reducing mean replay from 29.0 nodes to 26.62 nodes (~8.2% less replay). This does **not** establish an 8.2% production saving; it establishes the mechanism: a capture manifest may prove all live channels were mediated yet still be insufficient for selective rollback unless dependency capture can prove the affected closure is complete.

`conservative enlargement` is slightly cheaper in this run but accepts a residual `2/30,000` stale failures. That is useful as an efficiency baseline, not as evidence for a zero-stale destructive-repair policy.

## Omission detectability: what receipt chains can and cannot prove

The experiment and public sources support the following machine-contract distinction:

| condition | hash/sequence chain can detect? | extra contract needed |
|---|---|---|
| middle receipt omitted | yes, sequence/link gap | none beyond complete presented chain |
| duplicate sequence | yes | none |
| reordering / broken predecessor link | yes | none |
| tail withheld with authenticated checkpoint | yes, as checkpoint/head disagreement | authenticated checkpoint |
| tail withheld and checkpoint also withheld | **no general proof from presented chain** | external checkpoint/transparency/witness |
| unregistered wrapper/tool channel bypasses recorder | **no** | positive capture boundary / receiver or gateway mediation |
| topology split/merge occurs outside capture epoch | **no** from old manifest alone | mandatory mutation mediator + epoch rollover before live traffic |
| all channels mediated, but dependency edges are incomplete | chain can still be valid | positive dependency/descendant-closure completeness |

This is the main design correction from the prior run: **tamper evidence is not capture completeness, and capture completeness is not dependency-closure completeness**.

## Candidate machine-checkable `CaptureScopeContract`

The next concrete design target is a compact, verifier-friendly object with separate proof axes rather than a scalar “coverage” score:

```text
capture_epoch_id
topology_digest
surface_inventory[]
  surface_class
  channel_id
  mediation_mode
  completeness_claim   # proved / known_unmediated / unknown
  capture_adapter_version
  scope_digest
receipt_chain
  genesis
  highest_seq
  authenticated_close_checkpoint
dynamic_registration
  tool_registry_digest
  wrapper_registry_digest
  topology_mutation_receipt
dependency_contract
  edge_capture_mode
  per_surface_edge_completeness
  descendant_closure_completeness
external_evidence
  receiver_receipt_mode
  transparency_checkpoint
  unmatched_activity_sentinel
```

A local destructive replay should require a positive proof on the exact axis it relies on. A valid hash chain cannot satisfy a missing capture-scope claim; a complete surface inventory cannot satisfy a missing descendant-closure claim.

## Scope and negative evidence

- The 30-node DAG, probabilities, costs and surface mix are synthetic.
- `100%` in the closure-proof condition is guaranteed by the experiment's **truthful synthetic completeness contract** for the relevant surface classes; it is not evidence that present agent frameworks can already produce such proof.
- The experiment intentionally separates statistical recall from positive completeness. A real system that labels a 99% empirical capture rate as “complete” would violate the mechanism being tested.
- SCITT draft-01 is an Internet-Draft, not a final RFC, and its action receipt records what occurred at the governed boundary, not downstream effects.
- Notarized Agents is a preprint and explicitly retains suppression/collusion limits.
- ATM evaluates topology mutation safety/performance, not rollback provenance; it is used here only as evidence that structural mutation needs explicit invariants and pre-live validation.
