### CLG1-CL-005 delta — Infinite LR: optimizer reset is part of the reported protocol; dynamic replay ablation is composition+phase confounded
Primary source: Singh et al., CoLLAs/PMLR 2026.

Newly verified protocol detail:
- The large MAE experiments reset AdamW optimizer states before each task.
- The LLM experiments likewise reset optimizer states between datasets, explicitly motivated as common practice when starting from an open-weight model.
- No no-reset optimizer experiment was found in the paper. Therefore current evidence supports an LR-trajectory intervention under reset-between-domain protocols; it does not establish that the same benefit survives uninterrupted optimizer-state continuation.

Appendix F dynamic replay:
- During the German annealing phase, the normal replay composition is 50% current German + 25% DCLM + 25% Stack.
- The equalized variant changes this to 33.33% DCLM + 33.33% Stack + 33.33% German during annealing.
- Authors report improved prior-domain performance without deterioration on German.
- Scope warning: this is not a clean fixed-replay-rate timing ablation. It simultaneously changes *when* the change is applied and raises the old-domain share from 50% to 66.7% during annealing, so the gain cannot be attributed uniquely to late-phase timing.

Additional online/continual metrics from the paper:
- Cosine + ER(5%): Overall Acc 44.76, REM 97.11, BWT -2.89, FWT 12.85, CL score 37.96.
- Infinite cosine + ER(5%): 50.56, 99.54, -0.46, 12.78, 40.61.
- Cosine without ER: 43.61, 84.61, -15.39, 12.80, 31.41.
- Infinite without ER: 44.36, 86.98, -13.02, 13.08, 32.85.
These aggregate metrics strengthen the retention result and again show little evidence that the effect is mainly forward-transfer/plasticity.

Fourth-task medical continuation (ImageNet→Places2→FireRisk→MRI, 50% replay): Infinite vs repeated cosine after MRI is ImageNet 57.20 vs 54.11, Places2 47.16 vs 46.46, FireRisk 60.56 vs 60.32, MRI 85.92 vs 85.93. The current MRI task is essentially tied while older-domain retention improves, reinforcing the interpretation that the intervention mainly changes retention.

### CLG1-CL-008 delta — OASIS: text-only evidence confirmed; linked code repository is currently empty
Primary source: Lee et al., ACL 2026.

Method/computation reconciliation:
- Sec. 3, Algorithm 1, and appendix cost accounting explicitly compute per-sample **last-layer gradients** for Fisher-like informativeness and gradient-similarity redundancy control.
- The appendix explicitly groups OASIS with methods that require backward computation, although only for the final layer, and reports under ~3.5% overhead relative to a forward pass / roughly 3.4% more selection compute than forward-only baselines.
- Therefore the paper's separate “only a forward pass” / “inference-only” wording is internally inconsistent and should not be propagated as a method property.

Text-only evidence, LLaMA-3.1-8B, 6.25% selected samples:
- Long Sequence: OASIS Aavg 75.26±0.35 / Alast 71.91±0.55. Strong non-OASIS baselines are approximately 74.38±0.37 / 69.83±0.54 (InfoBatch) and 74.37±0.75 / 70.65±0.57 (DivBS). Full Data is 85.83±0.48 / 82.83±0.47.
- TRACE: OASIS 45.56±0.35 / 53.48±0.17. DivBS is 44.25±0.08 / 52.23±0.76; Random reaches 52.27±0.03 Alast. Full Data is 50.84±0.70 / 57.73±0.54.
This confirms the mechanism is not supported only by multimodal benchmarks, while also showing a substantial remaining gap to full-data training.

Task-free caveat:

### CLG1-CL-004 delta — 5-task replay remains strong, but 560M downstream averages are internally inconsistent in the primary paper
Primary source: Abbes et al., CoLLAs/PMLR 2026.

Protocol detail:
- English→French→German→Arabic→Japanese is used for the 1B five-task extension, with 100B tokens per language.
- Unlike the Infinite-LR study, Abbes et al. explicitly do **not** reset gradients/optimizer between tasks.
- Replay uses a disk-backed effectively unbounded buffer in the study; this is not a constrained-memory result.

Five-task extension:
- The five-task figure is a bar chart rather than a numeric table. The paper text states most methods incur more forgetting at five tasks, while replay-based methods remain strong; 50% replay + Reptile achieves negative forgetting.
- Because the figure lacks exact values, do not store visually estimated bar heights as exact quantitative evidence. Preserve only the qualitative primary claim unless machine-readable numbers are found.
- The paper itself lists the single-scale five-task experiment as a limitation; broader scale validation is still needed.

### New frontier lead — HESTIA: task-free order-invariant updates + density-guided routing without replay
Primary source: Le et al., UAI/PMLR 2026, `Task-Free Continual Learning via Order-Invariant Linearized Adaptation and Density-Guided Adapter Routing`.
Official code: `Alisia0303/HESTIA`.

Mechanism from primary abstract + official artifact:
- Linearize adaptation around the pretrained model so sufficient statistics accumulated over streaming batches are additive and updates become invariant to batch order, targeting optimization-order-induced forgetting without replay.
- Dynamically expand lightweight adapters as distributions shift.
- Associate each adapter with a Gaussian-mixture density key; use density signals for change-point detection during training and likelihood-based adapter retrieval at inference.
- The paper provides a retrieval-error characterization in terms of density-estimation quality and cross-adapter embedding separability.
- Evaluated on Split CIFAR-10/100, Split ImageNet-R, and VTAB5T; official code/configs and baseline implementations are public.

Status: high-priority provisional candidate because it directly covers the task-free/change-point frontier and separates within-regime order sensitivity from between-regime distribution routing. Do not promote it to strong quantitative status until exact paper tables/ablations are extracted and checked.

## Updated synthesis
1. **Optimizer-state protocol is a hidden axis in continual-learning comparisons.** Infinite-LR resets optimizer state per dataset; Abbes continues optimizer state across tasks. Apparent schedule/replay differences should not be compared as if optimizer-state handling were matched.
2. **Replay timing and replay composition remain confounded in some “dynamic replay” evidence.** Infinite-LR Appendix F improves retention after raising old-task share specifically during annealing; this does not isolate timing from composition.
3. **Task-free continual learning requires an explicit boundary-discovery/routing story, not just streaming statistics.** OASIS is online but not demonstrated task-free; HESTIA explicitly models change points and routing under unknown boundaries.
4. **Artifact availability should be graded separately from paper claims.** OASIS currently has an empty linked repository; Abbes and HESTIA have populated public implementations.
5. **Primary-paper internal consistency itself is part of evidence quality.** Abbes 560M downstream averages are internally contradictory and must remain excluded rather than silently repaired.
