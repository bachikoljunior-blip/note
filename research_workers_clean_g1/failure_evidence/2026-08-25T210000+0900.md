## New claims

### FE-025 — Controlled harness substitution can dominate model substitution and reverse model rankings on a fixed SWE-bench subset
**Status:** primary preprint/table verified; public implementation/config verified; raw final result CSVs not committed in the public repository, so reproducibility is incomplete.

Primary source: Yunbei Zhang et al., *Stop Comparing LLM Agents Without Disclosing the Harness*, Preprints.org v1, posted 2026-05-12. Public text: https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202605.0711 . Public code: https://github.com/yunbeizhang/harness-binding-constraint .

Matched design reported by the authors:
- 3 models: GLM-5.1, GPT-5.4, Kimi K2.6.
- 3 harnesses: H1 Minimal, H2 Improved, H3 Full.
- Same difficulty-stratified 100-task SWE-bench Verified subset, task order, Docker environment, evaluator, 50-step budget, and 120-second per-step timeout.
- Two final runs for every model × harness cell.
- Harness is the manipulated factor: context construction, tool schema/exposure, retry/error handling, validation, self-checking, drift/anomaly detection, checkpoint/rollback.

Reported mean pass@1 (%):
- GLM-5.1: H1 52.5, H2 56.5, H3 65.5 — **13.0 pp harness range**.
- GPT-5.4: H1 55.0, H2 58.5, H3 63.5 — **8.5 pp range**.
- Kimi K2.6: H1 52.0, H2 59.0, H3 60.5 — **8.5 pp range**.
- Mean harness-induced variance 18.48 pp² versus mean model-induced variance 2.37 pp², reported ratio **7.80×**.
- The paper reports **6 / 9 model-pair × harness-pair ranking reversals**.

The public repository corroborates the experimental structure: its README says all harnesses share task set, model configuration, Docker execution environment and SWE-bench evaluator, and contains the H1/H2/H3 configs plus the fixed `subset_100.json`. The complete repository tree does **not** contain the `results/` or `assets/paper_results_tables.csv` artifacts that `code/scripts/process_paper_results.py` explicitly expects/produces, so the published numerical table cannot currently be independently recomputed from committed raw final-run files alone.

**Failure implication, limited to tested scope:** on this 100-task / three-model / three-harness controlled grid, treating an agent benchmark score as a model-only property can produce the wrong ordering; harness choice was a larger source of variance than model choice. This does **not** establish a universal 7.80× ratio, and the authors explicitly do not claim universality.

**Evidence-strength caveat:** this is a 2026 concept-position preprint (not peer reviewed at the retrieved version) with a public runnable framework/configuration but missing committed final result CSVs. Preserve the table as primary-author evidence, not independent replication.

### FE-026 — Online memory/skill gains can disappear against a budget-aware vanilla control; equal nominal step count is a confounded baseline
**Status:** primary arXiv HTML/table verified; three-run matched comparisons; budget match is approximate rather than exact token equality.

Primary source: Sina Hajimiri et al., *Are Online Skill and Memory Modules Always Worth Their Tokens? A Budget-Constrained Study of Web Agents*, arXiv:2606.15017, submitted 2026-06-12; project page marks EMNLP 2026. Primary HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15017 .

Design:
- Compares AWM, ASI, and ReasoningBank with `Vanilla-IB`, a stateless vanilla actor that spends more of the inference budget on environment interaction (15-step horizon plus accessibility-tree pruning).
- WebArena Shopping/Reddit/Admin; Gemini 3 Flash, GPT-5.4-mini, Qwen 3.6-27B; **3 independent runs** per domain/model configuration.
- Reported success rate and *total* token usage include actor plus auxiliary module calls.

Aggregate WebArena success-rate means (%), averaged across the three reported domains:
- Gemini 3 Flash: **Vanilla-IB 50.74** vs AWM 44.98, ASI 47.86, ReasoningBank 45.54.
- GPT-5.4-mini: **Vanilla-IB 36.63** vs AWM 30.74, ASI 32.14, ReasoningBank 28.42.
- Qwen 3.6-27B: **Vanilla-IB 47.44** vs AWM 43.58, ASI 45.61, ReasoningBank 43.09.

Representative matched cost/result cells:
- Gemini Shopping: Vanilla-IB **47.77±1.9% at 45.7±0.8K tokens/task** vs AWM 41.18±2.1% at 70.8±3.0K, ASI 44.74±1.2% at 82.8±0.3K, ReasoningBank 45.45±1.4% at 54.6±1.3K.
- Gemini Reddit: Vanilla-IB and AWM tie at 47.48% mean success, but Vanilla-IB uses 71.7K vs AWM 87.2K tokens/task. In a horizon sweep, the authors report Vanilla-IB surpasses AWM by larger horizons while remaining below AWM token use through 25 steps.
- WorkArena-L1 under Qwen 3.6-27B: Vanilla-IB 55.56±2.9% / 109.4±14.8K tokens; ReasoningBank 55.56±2.8% / 120.3±5.7K; AWM 53.53±3.8% / 102.8±2.6K; ASI 48.49±4.3% / 113.8±10.2K.

**Critical scope/caveat:** `Vanilla-IB` is **not an exact per-task token-matched treatment**. The paper states that augmented methods have different aggregate budgets and module/actor splits, so 15 actor steps are a practical approximation; in a small number of tasks Vanilla-IB spends slightly more than one or more augmented methods. Therefore the defensible conclusion is that same-step baselines can overstate augmentation gains and that these particular online augmentation methods do not dominate a budget-aware vanilla actor in the tested settings—not that memory/skills are generally useless or that an exact equal-compute causal effect has been isolated.

### FE-027 — Judge-gated online memory/skill construction can store failure-derived guidance as “successful” state
**Status:** primary quantitative failure-mode tables verified; same study as FE-026, so treat as mechanism evidence within those implementations rather than independent replication.

Primary source: arXiv:2606.15017, Appendix D / Tables 6–8: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15017 .

**ASI verification confound (WebArena):** the induced high-level function is called first in a verification episode; if it fails but the agent recovers with primitive actions, the episode can still be judged correct and the function stored.
- GPT-5.4-mini Shopping: 44.3 average induction attempts/run; 32.0 first-step failures = **72.2%**; **54.2% of those failures recovered**, allowing a potentially broken function to enter the library.
- Gemini 3 Flash Shopping: 58.0 attempts; 19.3 first-step failures ≈ **33.3%**; **69.0% recovery** among those failures.

**AWM induction contamination:** fraction of workflow-induction events originating from trajectories the ground-truth evaluator classified as failed:
- Shopping: **49.5% Gemini**, **52.3% GPT-5.4-mini**.
- Admin: 42.1% Gemini, 42.0% GPT.
- Reddit lower: 10.9% Gemini, 19.1% GPT.

**ReasoningBank label contamination:** among entries labeled successful by its judge, fraction whose trajectories failed the ground-truth evaluator:
- Shopping: **52.9% Gemini**, **59.5% GPT**.
- Reddit: 30.2% Gemini, 60.0% GPT.
- Admin: 42.6% Gemini, 48.1% GPT.

The paper attributes the shared mechanism to judge inputs lacking intermediate page-state/action evidence. That attribution is plausible but is an author diagnosis, not a randomized intervention proving the judge-input gap is the sole cause.

**Failure implication, limited to these implementations/environments:** a self-improvement or memory system can create persistent contamination when its quality-control judge validates the *episode outcome* rather than the contribution/correctness of the artifact being learned. Later retrieval can then propagate guidance whose success label is false with respect to the benchmark’s ground-truth evaluator.

## Run-to-run instability detail retained from FE-026/027 source
The same paper quantifies within-task instability on WebArena Shopping across three runs. For GPT-5.4-mini Vanilla-IB, three-run mean success is 38.50%, while Any-of-3 is 46.52% and All-of-3 is 27.81% (gaps +8.0 / −10.7 pp). For GPT AWM, mean 29.95%, Any-of-3 37.43%, All-of-3 21.39%. These are task-level OR/AND bounds, **not** independent estimates of deployment pass@k; they nevertheless show that a single aggregate run hides a material set of task-level flips in this setup.
