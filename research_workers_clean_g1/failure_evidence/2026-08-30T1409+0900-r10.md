### Result

Issue #3390 is titled `push: schema + controller stamps outbound commands with the Raft term` and is closed with `state_reason=completed` as of 2026-08-23. Its own scope is explicit: add a `term` field to the outbound `Command`, make the controller publisher stamp the current Raft term, and wire the HA manager as the term source. But the same issue states that **steward-side enforcement is out of scope**, naming #3436 for rejecting stale terms.
