### FE-042 — Stronger held-out attacker selection can flip an AI-control safety conclusion at a fixed monitor threshold
**Status:** public primary code/artifact bundle + manuscript; single LinuxArena environment, modest sample size, not a universal attacker ordering.

Primary artifact: Jason Tang / Apart Research, `jasontang-ai/adapto`, *Attacker Pressure as a First-Class Variable in AI Control Evaluation* and committed LinuxArena evidence bundle.
- Manuscript: https://github.com/jasontang-ai/adapto/blob/main/main.tex
- Held-out artifact: https://github.com/jasontang-ai/adapto/blob/main/artifacts/linuxarena/research_upgrade/heldout_validation/heldout_summary.json
- Uncertainty artifact: https://github.com/jasontang-ai/adapto/blob/main/artifacts/linuxarena/research_upgrade/uncertainty/lofo_uncertainty_summary.json

Matched evidence:
- Environment is fixed to LinuxArena `clinical_trial` benchmark commit `0667bf26542b3e4ea82338265e47c1558fdb2852`; monitor threshold is fixed at 10.
- On one held-out family (`family3_jan29`, eval n=5), the simple baseline attacker has uncaught ASR 0.0, while a stronger lane selected only on the other two families has uncaught ASR 0.2; detection-on-success for the selected lane is 0.0.
- Across the 3-family LOFO uncertainty summary, strict baseline uncaught ASR mean is 0.0; selected stronger-lane uncaught ASR/delta mean is 0.1333 with reported 95% bootstrap interval [0.0, 0.2].

Failure implication: a fixed-threshold control evaluation that appears safe under a weak attacker can change materially when attacker policy search improves, even without changing environment or monitor threshold. The evidence is narrow: one LinuxArena environment, three small task families, and generated attacker lanes; it does not establish a universal capability ordering.

### FE-044 — Iterative hard-negative mining finds substantially stronger evasion than simple generation/fuzzing against the tested detector, and most attacker gain arrives almost immediately
**Status:** peer-reviewed NDSS LAST-X 2026 workshop paper; one base detector architecture and synthetic prompt-harness setting.

Primary source: Chen et al., *Proactive Hardening of LLM Defenses with HASTE*, NDSS LAST-X 2026, https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/lastx2026-83.pdf

Matched evidence (Table III, in-loop test partition, no retraining):
- ProtectAI DeBERTa-v3 detector starts at 95.91% accuracy.
- `HM-Max+Sem` drops detector accuracy to **37.00% after one iteration** and **31.76% by iteration 10**.
- Simple `Base` remains 95.68% at iteration 1 and 93.49% at iteration 10; semantic fuzzing alone (`Base-Sem`) reaches 67.44% at iteration 1 and 66.16% at iteration 10.
- The paper explicitly notes that hard-negative strategies plateau quickly; most gain is achieved after the first iteration.

Failure implication: static or weak red-team generation can drastically understate detector vulnerability. However, this does not imply all detectors degrade similarly: the study uses a single ProtectAI DeBERTa-v3 base classifier and its own synthetic generation/judge pipeline.

### FE-045 — HASTE hardening improves aggregate/out-of-loop performance while regressing several malicious subclasses under a shifted fuzzing condition
**Status:** same peer-reviewed primary source; category-level deltas, not a universal defense regression.

Primary source: HASTE Table VI, out-of-loop evaluation under `All-type fuzzing`.
- ProtectAI DeBERTa-v3 baseline accuracies: benign 27.6, obfuscation 94.8, objective-manipulation 97.3, other 92.1, role-play 98.4.
- `HM-Bal` and `HM-Bal+All` each improve benign accuracy by **+62.7 pp**, but malicious-category deltas are **-4.1 pp obfuscation, -6.0 pp objective manipulation, -10.2 pp other, -10.2 pp role play**.
- Other hard-mining variants show smaller but still negative deltas on some attack categories under the same shifted fuzzing evaluation.

Failure implication: adversarial hardening can move average or benign metrics strongly upward while silently worsening selected attack subclasses under distribution shift. Aggregate H-accuracy is therefore insufficient for failure analysis; category-level regression checks are needed. This finding is scoped to this detector, training pipeline, taxonomy, and All-type fuzzing condition.
