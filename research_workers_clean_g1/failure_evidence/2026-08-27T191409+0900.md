## New failure evidence

### FE-242 — Retrying an ambiguous tool failure without checking world state can duplicate side effects at very high rates; postcondition verification changes the outcome without changing the LLM
**Primary source:**
- Mansoor, Phadke & Rana, “Verified Tool Calls Improve LLM Agent Reliability Under Non-Atomic Failures,” arXiv:2608.02645: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02645

**Public result transcription used for exact table cells:**
- Daniel Vaughan, technical reading of the paper’s reported tables: https://codex.danielvaughan.com/2026/08/13/verified-tool-calls-non-atomic-failures-llm-agent-reliability-codex-cli-posttooluse-postcondition-verification/

The primary study keeps the underlying language model fixed and injects non-atomic tool failures such as timeout-after-dispatch, delayed visibility, partial success and stale conflicts in a controlled simulator. The proposed wrapper checks an independent postcondition, verifies before retrying, and uses idempotency keys rather than treating the tool response as a faithful oracle of the resulting world state. The paper’s abstract reports a significant reduction in duplicate actions while maintaining comparable task success.

A public transcription of the paper’s reported experiment tables gives the magnitude for two tasks over `300` runs: at the high fault setting, retry-only `Activate Customer` produces duplicate effects in `72%` of runs and succeeds in `64%`; the wrapped design reduces duplicates to `20%` and reaches `100%` success. For `Record Invoice`, duplicates are reported as `76%→20%` while success is `96%→100%`. The same transcription reports that verification alone captures most of the duplicate-reduction benefit, while retry logic mainly recovers completion once state has been checked.

**Failure implication:** “the call timed out/errored” and “the side effect did not happen” are different claims. A reasoning loop that blindly retries can convert uncertainty in the response channel into duplicated external state. For mutating tools, verify-before-retry and idempotency are system semantics, not optional prompt advice.

**Scope/provenance guard:** the exact percentage cells above are taken from a public technical transcription of the paper’s tables because the currently accessible primary arXiv abstract/HTML view exposes the qualitative result but not those table cells directly. Treat the exact numbers as secondary-extracted until independently checked against the paper PDF/source. The primary experiment is a controlled simulator with two task templates and a fixed model; it does not establish production duplicate rates or eliminate eventual-consistency/verification-race problems in arbitrary APIs.
