### FE-144 — In AdvGame, weaker/fixed adversarial pressure and locally favorable training signals can coexist with materially worse external safety
**Status:** primary paper + official Meta code release; Qwen2.5-7B DPO ablation, exact tested setup only.

Primary sources:
- Meta AI, *Safety Alignment of LMs via Non-cooperative Games*, https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/safety-alignment-of-lms-via-non-cooperative-games/
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20806
- Official code: https://github.com/facebookresearch/advgame

Evidence from the paper's Qwen2.5-7B DPO ablation table:
- Full `AdvGame-DPO-MD`: IFBench `30.7`, ArenaHard `61.3`, HarmBench ASR `4.7`, WJB ASR `8.5`, DAN ASR `10.3`.
- Replacing the trained/adaptive attacker with a **fixed attacker** leaves utility close (`29.9`, `61.8`) but worsens WJB `8.5→16.6` and DAN `10.3→15.0`; HarmBench changes `4.7→5.1`.
- Replacing the pairwise judge with a **point-wise judge** worsens HarmBench `4.7→14.1`, WJB `8.5→30.8`, DAN `10.3→20.7`, while ArenaHard falls `61.3→56.3`.
- Removing EMA/off-policy sampling (`no EMA`) worsens HarmBench `4.7→19.9`, WJB `8.5→49.5`, DAN `10.3→25.0`; IFBench also falls `30.7→27.3`.
- The paper explicitly warns that the point-wise-judge variant can show apparently better validation rewards for attacker/defender while downstream safety is worse because the validation reward itself is produced by the changed judge.
- The official repository contains distinct paper configs for the main Qwen2.5 DPO setup and ablations including pointwise/on-policy/no-judge/nonoptimistic variants, supporting that these are implemented experiment branches rather than prose-only suggestions.

Failure implication: low in-training attack success or favorable local reward is not sufficient evidence of external robustness when the adversarial pressure, judge, or sampling distribution is part of the learned game. External held-out safety evaluation is required, and fixed-attacker evaluation can overstate robustness.

Scope guard: this does **not** establish that fixed attackers, point-wise judges, on-policy training, or absence of EMA are generally inferior. The causal statement is limited to the paper's Qwen2.5-7B AdvGame DPO ablations, datasets, reward/judge construction and training recipe. Multiple components differ in some rows, so the table should not be read as a universal isolated-effect estimate.

Scope guard: absence of the real-LLM code/data in the current repository is **not evidence that the reported result is false**. It is a provenance/reproduction limitation.
