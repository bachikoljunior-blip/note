The strongest source found is Chidori's deployment documentation, which is useful negative evidence but remains design/documentation evidence rather than the required matched incident. Chidori explicitly distinguishes backends: deploy-overlap leases are genuine compare-and-swap on `sqlite`, `chidori cell-store`, and its Durable Object relay, while `fs` and `s3://` are described as advisory/last-writer-wins and therefore require stopping the old instance first (`Recreate`, not rolling).
