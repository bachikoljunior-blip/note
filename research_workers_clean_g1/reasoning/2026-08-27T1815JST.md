## C508 — Lemma eligibility and lemma utility should be separate stages

RULES (`Reliable Use of Lemmas via Eligibility Reasoning and Section-Aware Reinforcement Learning`, ACL 2026 short paper / arXiv:2602.00998) explicitly decomposes lemma judging into a precondition check and a conclusion-utility check, with the final useful decision requiring both. It trains on several math/formal corpora including Isabelle premise-selection data and reports stronger robustness to applicability-breaking perturbations, while end-to-end gains are mixed/parity rather than universal.

Design implication for formal-library routing: `LemmaUtilityPriorV0` should not conflate applicability with usefulness. Prefer exact/mechanical applicability evidence where the formal environment can provide it; learned applicability may prioritize unresolved cases but cannot bypass the kernel. Utility is evaluated only after the applicability stage.

Scope: RULES is a lemma-judging model spanning natural-language and formal corpora, not an end-to-end Lean library-admission system. Its strongest evidence is robustness of check-before-use, not causal future library utility.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00998

## C511 — Low-budget permutation Shapley can miss a rare but real bridge contribution

The five-premise fixture was extended with a uniform random-permutation Shapley estimator and evaluated over 10,000 deterministic seeds at each sampling budget. Exact bridge credit for `e_P_implies_Q` is `1/30`, but the sampled estimate was exactly zero in:
- 87.03% of 4-permutation runs;
- 76.11% of 8-permutation runs;
- 58.20% of 16-permutation runs;
- 34.00% of 32-permutation runs;
- 11.60% of 64-permutation runs;
- 1.46% of 128-permutation runs.

Overall median RMSE against exact Shapley fell from 0.1871 at 4 permutations to 0.0308 at 128, but tail error remained nontrivial at low budgets.

Design implication: an estimated zero from a small coalition sample must not be treated as evidence of zero contribution. Support-credit events should carry sampling budget and uncertainty; use minimum/adaptive sampling before elimination. Exact coalition evaluation remains preferable for very small frozen candidate universes.

Durable result: `research_workers_clean_g1/reasoning/fixtures/support_family_v0.sampled_shapley.json`.

Scope: synthetic five-player calibration only.
