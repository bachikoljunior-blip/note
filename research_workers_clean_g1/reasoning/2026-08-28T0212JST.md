## C557 — The upper Shapley-completion LP collapses to two extremal monotone slices

For a fixed player `i`, write `a(T)=v(T)` and `b(T)=v(T∪{i})` on coalitions `T⊆N\{i}`. For a monotone simple game, both slices are isotone and `a<=b` pointwise. The Shapley-Shubik index is

`phi_i = sum_T w_|T| (b(T)-a(T))`, where `w_k=k!(n-k-1)!/n! = 1/(n*C(n-1,k))`.

Under point observations consistent with at least one monotone completion, define:
- `A1`: observed `T` not containing `i` with value 1;
- `B0`: observed `T∪{i}` with value 0.

The pointwise-minimum feasible lower slice is `a_min=1` exactly on the up-closure of `A1`; the pointwise-maximum feasible upper slice is `b_max=0` exactly on the down-closure of `B0`. Any feasible completion `a*,b*` satisfies `a_min<=a*<=b*<=b_max`, so `a_min<=b_max`. Therefore `(a_min,b_max)` itself is a feasible monotone completion and simultaneously minimizes every negative objective term and maximizes every positive objective term. Hence it attains the exact maximum Shapley value. No maximum-flow optimization is needed for this upper bound.

Equivalently, only two boundary antichains matter: maximal elements of `B0` and minimal elements of `A1`.

## C558 — Exact upper bound is rank-weighted model counting of an uncertainty family

Let

`R_i = B_{n-1} \ (down(B0_max) union up(A1_min))`.

Then the exact maximum is

`phi_i^max = (1/n) * sum_{k=0}^{n-1} |R_i ∩ C(n-1,k)| / C(n-1,k)`.

So the certifier is exactly the average, over coalition-size ranks, of the fraction of coalitions that are not yet forced to be non-pivotal by observations.

`R_i` has a direct CNF encoding:
- for each maximal observed upper-slice zero `Z`, require `OR_{j notin Z} x_j`;
- for each minimal observed lower-slice one `A`, require `OR_{j in A} not x_j`.

Thus exact certification reduces to cardinality-aware model counting for this boundary CNF. A memoized ordered-decision-diagram DP was implemented in `experiments/coalition_boundary_bdd_certifier_v0.py`.

Independent validation:
- exhaustive `n=3`: all 20 monotone Boolean functions × all 256 observation subsets × 3 players = **15,360** cases; direct enumeration over all feasible monotone completions matched the boundary formula with maximum absolute error **0**;
- random `n=3..8`: **1,080** cases matched the explicit maximum-closure/max-flow formulation to at most `1.11e-16` (BDD) / `1.44e-15` (closed boundary enumeration).

## C561 — Compression target shifts from maximum closure to exact weighted counting of the boundary CNF

The previous frontier emphasized BDD/ZDD or branch-and-cut compression of an explicit maximum-closure problem. C557 shows that, for this exact *upper-Shapley under monotone point observations* problem, the optimization layer can be eliminated before compression. The remaining hard object is the uncertainty set family `R_i` and its rank counts.

This aligns with established decision-diagram literature without implying that the exact boundary formula is already known there:
- maximum closure is classically equivalent to min-cut: Hochbaum, 2001, https://doi.org/10.1002/net.1012
- ZDDs are designed to compactly represent sparse families of sets; Minato project overview: https://www.jst.go.jp/erato/minato/
- chain-reduced BDD/ZDD representations can inherit compactness properties across BDD/ZDD forms: Bryant, `Chain Reduction for Binary and Zero-Suppressed Decision Diagrams`, arXiv:1710.06500
- exact weighted model counting with algebraic decision diagrams is an established route for symbolic counting: Dudek, Phan, Vardi, `ADDMC`, arXiv:1907.05000

A targeted search found literature on incomplete cooperative games and Shapley values, but no source in this pass stating this exact partial-observation monotone-simple-game boundary formula. Do not treat absence in this search as a novelty claim.
