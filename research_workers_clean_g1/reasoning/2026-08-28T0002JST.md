## C545 — Shapley credit can be reparameterized into ordinary finite-population means instead of dependent vertex-edge HT uncertainty

For player `i`, group predecessor coalitions by size `k`. Maleki et al. explicitly define the stratum mean
`phi_k = mean_{|S|=k, i notin S}[v(S union {i}) - v(S)]`
and show `phi_i = (1/n) * sum_{k=0}^{n-1} phi_k` (arXiv:1306.4265, equations 8–9 in the public text).

This means the uncertainty problem does not have to be attacked through the current vertex-sampling design, where a marginal edge is observed only when both endpoint coalitions happen to be sampled and edge indicators are dependent. An alternative is to sample the **marginal contributions themselves** uniformly without replacement inside each `(player, coalition-size)` finite population and aggregate the stratum means equally.

Scope: this is an estimator/design change. It does not prove the new design is more oracle-efficient than the prior vertex-HT point estimator; that is now an explicit experiment.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4265

## C546 — Valid sequential uncertainty is already available for the reparameterized design

Waudby-Smith & Ramdas provide confidence sequences for finite-population means under sampling without replacement, including Hoeffding/empirical-Bernstein style time-uniform constructions; their reported applications include Shapley-value estimation. Bardenet & Maillard independently provide strengthened Serfling/Bernstein concentration results for sampling without replacement.

Therefore a defensible safe-elimination route is:
1. sample marginal contributions without replacement within each `(i,k)` stratum;
2. maintain a valid one-sided WoR bound/CS for each stratum mean;
3. combine the upper bounds as `U_i = (1/n) sum_k U_{i,k}` with a predeclared familywise allocation across players/strata, or use a jointly valid construction;
4. certify only when `U_i <= epsilon`.

This avoids importing an unproved variance formula for the dependent-edge Horvitz–Thompson estimator. It may be more conservative or more expensive in unique value-oracle calls, which is why the preregistered experiment measures cost-to-certification as well as RMSE.

Sources:
- Waudby-Smith & Ramdas, *Confidence Sequences for Sampling Without Replacement*, NeurIPS 2020: https://mlanthology.org/neurips/2020/waudbysmith2020neurips-confidence/
- Bardenet & Maillard, *Concentration inequalities for sampling without replacement*, Bernoulli 2015 / arXiv:1309.4029.

## C547 — Exact-zero demotion and statistically negligible demotion are different targets

In the current monotone binary support games, Shapley marginal contributions lie in `[0,1]`. If any unsampled marginal can still be positive under a game consistent with the observations, incomplete sampling cannot in general certify the logical statement `phi_i = 0` merely because every observed marginal was zero. A valid upper confidence bound can instead certify the statistical statement `phi_i <= epsilon` at a predeclared error level. Exact-zero demotion therefore remains an **exact-completion or structurally proved** decision unless stronger assumptions are added.

This refines the previous sampled-zero guard: the missing ingredient is not just a better point estimator. The decision target must state whether it is certifying exact zero or practical negligibility.

Scope: this is a finite-identifiability argument for the current black-box marginal-oracle setting, not a theorem that no structured game can certify zero early.
