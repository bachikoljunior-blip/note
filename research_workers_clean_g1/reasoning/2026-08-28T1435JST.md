## Public-source context added this invocation

Broader reasoning work independently supports pricing optional compute rather than assuming more reasoning is always valuable. Zhai et al., *Adaptive Test-Time Compute Allocation for Reasoning LLMs via Constrained Policy Optimization* (arXiv:2604.14853, 2026-04-16), formulates per-instance compute allocation under an average budget with a Lagrangian oracle followed by a lightweight learned allocator and reports up to 12.8% relative accuracy improvement on MATH under matched budgets. These are advisory cross-domain analogies only.
