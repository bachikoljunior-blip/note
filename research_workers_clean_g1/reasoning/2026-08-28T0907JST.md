### C600 — Current public Lean routing code independently supports trajectory-aware cost/quality control, but also suggests a cleaner feature/action formulation than the failed synthetic classifier

The public implementation accompanying Rögnvaldsson et al., *Optimizing the Cost-Quality Tradeoff of Agentic Theorem Provers in Lean* (`eth-sri/optimizing-lean-agents`, inspected at public commit `64e2736133f3d5faf44adc6861540dcb77bda9ec`) exposes a genuine control-plane/data-plane separation for Lean proof search.

Relevant implementation contracts:
- `proof_simulation/policies/cost_quality.py`: real `PROVE/CORRECT` actions are scored as `p - lambda * c`; `DECOMPOSE/CREATE_BREAKDOWN` form a neutral fallback; `TERMINATE` is selected only when no positive real action/decomposition remains. The code retains decision metadata containing estimated probability, cost, score, oracle probability for diagnostics, and supports a forced hot-start sequence before cost-quality routing.
- `proof_simulation/policies/quality/trajectory_logistic.py`: learns per-step success probabilities from recorded trajectories and reads state-tracked features at action time rather than judging only the static theorem.
- The published example pipeline tracks `normalized_similarity`, `error_diversity`, and `attempt_count`; the one-stage training config uses `normalized_similarity`, `error_diversity`, and `1/x:num_attempts` rather than a large collection of absolute problem-size features.
- `error_diversity.py` explicitly tracks unique compiler-error messages divided by total errors; `normalized_similarity.py` tracks normalized tactic-code similarity across attempts.

The arXiv paper reports that the action router observes failed Lean attempts, estimates success probability and cost, and decides whether to continue the current target or restart from a new breakdown; on a PutnamBench subset it reports a 25.8% average cost reduction versus fixed-step while preserving performance. This is direct formal-proof evidence that failed verifier-grounded trajectories contain actionable allocation signal.

The comparison is informative rather than causal for our synthetic experiment: their action space is `prove/correct/decompose/terminate`, not BDD ordering. But it reinforces two design corrections after C599: (1) prefer **behavioral trajectory features with natural normalization** over raw scale-dependent structural magnitudes when transferring across problem sizes; and (2) judge a learned controller on **incremental cost-quality value over a strong simple policy**, not merely on classification AUC or aggregate success dominated by obvious cases.

## Updated synthesis

The strongest result remains architectural, not statistical: **finish and retain a verified incumbent, then spend optional compute on challengers without ever discarding recall.** That converts exploration from a quality-risk decision into a cost-of-information/value-of-computation decision. The v1 staged controller already showed a preregistered 76.74% recovery of exhaustive challenger gain at 26.37% exhaustive challenger compute on untouched n=16 with zero harm. But n=18 showed that useful improvements can arrive only after hundreds of apparently poor prefix evaluations, so no short-prefix gap or low model probability is a negative certificate.

The first learned tail model should now be considered **retired as a policy candidate**: aggregate confirmation looked strong, but it was decision-equivalent to v1 on one holdout and its only identified n=18 differential action was a high-confidence false positive. This is precisely the failure mode that strict disagreement testing and out-of-distribution confirmation are meant to expose.

The next learned controller should therefore estimate incremental continuation value with scale-normalized, verifier-grounded trajectory features, horizon/cost information, explicit uncertainty/OOD detection, and a nonzero exploration floor. Its target should be `expected future verified gain - continuation cost` relative to the incumbent/simple-policy action, not `P(any eventual gain)` in isolation.
