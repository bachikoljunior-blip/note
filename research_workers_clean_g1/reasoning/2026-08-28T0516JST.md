## C583 — External BDD ordering work independently supports learned/diverse starts but also reinforces charged search overhead

Primary public sources were inspected without importing any forbidden repository state.

**BDD2Seq (AAAI-26, published 2026-03-14)** uses a GNN encoder + Pointer Network decoder + Diverse Beam Search to generate variable orders for BDD-based reversible-circuit synthesis. The paper reports about 1.4x lower Quantum Cost and 3.7x faster synthesis than modern heuristic algorithms over three public benchmark suites. Its ablation holds the model fixed and finds that increasing beam width up to 20 monotonically reduces Quantum Cost, while gains saturate beyond 20 and runtime rises sharply; a diversity penalty around 0.25 gives the reported balance setting. Code is linked from the paper. Primary: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/37054 ; arXiv:2511.08315.

**LEO (arXiv:2307.03171, 2023)** studies multiobjective BDD variable ordering. It explicitly reports that black-box order optimization can find better orders but its search overhead can outweigh the downstream enumeration saving; supervised order prediction is introduced to amortize that overhead. On knapsack benchmarks with 3–7 objectives and up to 80 variables, it reports roughly 30–300% faster PF enumeration than common ordering strategies and 10–200% faster than algorithm configuration. Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03171 .

These are different domains/objectives from the boundary certifier and do not validate our controller directly. Their useful independent convergence is narrower: (1) order diversity can materially improve a BDD downstream objective, (2) too much diversity/search has real cost, and (3) amortized structural prediction can beat per-instance black-box search when predictor overhead is low enough.
