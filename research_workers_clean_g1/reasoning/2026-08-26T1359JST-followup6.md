### C160 — feature-schema freezing before the provider pilot prevents an otherwise subtle adaptive-analysis failure

The feature manifest was frozen **before** observing provider-enabled Stage-A trajectories or policy-quality labels. This matters because adding/removing features after looking at held-out cost/solve behavior can turn controller design into hidden model selection over the evaluation set.

Allowed evolution after collection:

- bug fixes that can be applied from the lossless raw journal, assigned a new manifest id/hash and re-evaluated under a fresh split/protocol;
- prespecified ablations such as text/embedding augmentation, each with its own manifest and correction/reporting plan.

Not allowed under the original v0 claim:

- selecting features based on test-set controller gains and still reporting them as if v0 were preregistered;
- fitting category vocabularies or normalization on validation/test;
- making unsupported missing values numerically look like low cost.

### C161 — the manifest intentionally separates provenance compatibility from predictive compatibility

`estimator_version`, IDs, exact batch provenance and raw semantic text are retained in the causal event stream but excluded from v0 predictive inputs. Therefore two raw traces can remain fully auditable even if a future model cannot legitimately share a feature representation across them.

Dataset construction should check both:

1. raw event-schema compatibility;
2. feature-manifest/substrate compatibility.

A trace with a different cost-estimator semantics may be valid evidence but should not automatically be pooled into one v0 model merely because its JSON fields deserialize.
