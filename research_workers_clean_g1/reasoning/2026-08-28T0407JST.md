## C571 — TDD can support the required exact rank distribution, not only total model count

The TDD authors describe TDDs as a deterministic special case of **smooth structured DNNF** over a vtree; a node is a deterministic disjunction of pairs of child nodes, and the formal paper/slides state tractable model counting. Source: Capelli et al., SAT 2026, https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.SAT.2026.10 ; slides: https://florent.capelli.me/talks/2605-Dagstuhl/ .

Algebraic Model Counting establishes that AMC tasks can be evaluated on sd-DNNF circuits. Source: Kimmig, Van den Broeck & De Raedt, `Algebraic Model Counting`, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jal.2016.11.031 .

Therefore the exact Hamming-weight/rank vector needed by the present Shapley-bound certifier can be obtained on a TDD by evaluating over the polynomial semiring `N[z]/(z^(n+1))`, assigning each positive literal weight `z` and each negative literal weight `1`. Every satisfying model contributes exactly `z^(#true variables)`, so the final coefficient vector is the exact rank-count vector. At a decomposable AND, multiply/convolve child polynomials; at a deterministic smooth OR, add them. With dense coefficient arrays the direct implementation is polynomial in TDD size and `n` (naively quadratic in local scope for each convolution).

This is a derivation from the published TDD structure plus AMC, not a reported TDD benchmark. It closes the earlier conceptual concern that “tractable total model count” might be insufficient for exact cardinality-by-rank queries, while leaving practical compiler/runtime cost unmeasured.

## C572 — Switching from OBDD to TDD does not remove compilation-schedule control

The TDD authors’ own bottom-up pseudocode is clause-at-a-time: initialize `TDD(1,T)`, then for each clause apply its TDD and minimize. Their complexity is expressed through the maximum width of the clause-prefix functions `F_i = C_1 ∧ ... ∧ C_i`. Thus even though TDD can avoid OBDD succinctness barriers on bounded-treewidth CNFs, **prefix/schedule choice remains an explicit cost channel** in the published compiler architecture.

This does **not** transfer De Colnet’s OBDD/incremental lower bound automatically to TDD; it only establishes that TDD’s current bottom-up compilation cost is prefix-sensitive and therefore a balanced/recompile experiment remains meaningful.
