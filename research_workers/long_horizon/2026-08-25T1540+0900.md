### 1. OSL-MR: retention is a sequential resource-allocation problem, not a per-turn relevance score
Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10616
Primary HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10616v6
Mechanism: model memory retention under a hard budget as a partially observable sequential decision problem with delayed costs for future miss, reacquisition, and stale use. Enforce strict separation between online-observable features available when the retention decision is made and offline supervision such as gold evidence/answer content that becomes available only afterward. Use an online-safe Mixed-Score cold-start heuristic, log interactions, then train a frozen evidence-value learner from realized evidence membership.

Key evidence:
- On LoCoMo budget 128, OSL-MR reaches precision 0.201, recall 0.561, F1 0.263, occupancy 0.828, shifted reward 246.209. The strongest heuristic Mixed-Score is precision 0.051, recall 0.401, F1 0.087, occupancy 0.988, reward 120.476. A behavior-cloning learner reaches F1 0.184 and reward 184.899.
- On LongMemEval budget 256, OSL-MR reaches precision 0.382, recall 0.617, F1 0.401, occupancy 0.768, reward 1347.546 vs behavior-cloning precision 0.220, recall 0.474, F1 0.258, occupancy 0.891, reward 1070.364.
- At LongMemEval budget 1024, OSL-MR remains ahead of BC: F1 0.300 vs 0.257 while using lower occupancy 0.769 vs 0.812.
- The Mixed-Score prior is a real but smaller contributor. Removing only that scalar prior at LongMemEval budget 256 lowers F1 0.401 -> 0.382 and raises occupancy 0.768 -> 0.794.
- Giving the heuristic an undeployable gold-answer-overlap feature barely helps: LoCoMo budget128 F1 0.087 -> 0.095, still far below OSL-MR 0.263; LongMemEval budget1024 0.059 -> 0.061 vs OSL-MR 0.300.

Controlled optimality evidence:
- When future demand is stationary and temporal coupling disappears, deployable policies are within ~2% of the exact dynamic-programming optimum.
- Under shifting demand/costly reacquisition, the exact *single-step* optimizer can still be 31.5%/19.3% from the multi-step optimum.
- Learned OSL-MR closes this to about 10.1%/5.7%, ahead of hand-tuned Mixed-Score 11.3%/6.6% and comparable to explicit Lookahead-4 12.3%/5.9%, despite doing no online planning.
- Paper reports OSL-MR remains the strongest non-oracle method across broad 0.25x–4x sensitivity changes in penalty/reward coefficients.

Judgment: VERY HIGH conceptual relevance to O's Context Kernel. A routing/retention choice should be evaluated by its *future opportunity cost*, not only current-query relevance. Three concrete implications:
1. **Do not train/evaluate retention with information unavailable at decision time.** O's context-policy evaluator must distinguish deployable observables from hindsight-only labels to avoid leakage.
2. **Eviction has delayed costs.** If context can be reacquired from a durable raw store, the latency/tool cost of reacquisition belongs in the objective alongside stale-memory risk and active-context cost.
3. **Myopic optimal selection can still be globally poor.** Even a perfect one-step selector can fail under demand shifts; learned future-use value can approximate deeper planning cheaply.

## Cross-paper synthesis update

OSL-MR complements MESA/HIPIF/ACON rather than duplicating them:
- **MESA** chooses which structural views to materialize for the current query.
- **HIPIF** folds completed subgoals to control active context during execution.
- **ACON** learns compression rules from failures caused by lost context.
- **OSL-MR** asks the upstream question of which stored items should survive over time given future demand, reacquisition cost, and stale risk.

This suggests treating O context management as multiple separable policies: durable retention, structural organization, current materialization/routing, and behavioral intervention. A failure should first be attributed to the correct layer before modifying all of them.

## Evidence-scope cautions
- LoCoMo/LongMemEval are primarily memory QA/conversation benchmarks; MemoryArena already warns that retrieval quality can overestimate action-useful persistence. OSL-MR's *sequential retention formulation* is more transferable than its raw F1.
- Semantic staleness is only partially observable; OSL-MR uses proxies. O's explicit revision/event ledger may allow stronger deterministic freshness signals for some state.
- Evidence-membership labels are tractable for QA; O will need task-specific proxies such as whether an artifact/source was actually causal to a later verified decision.
