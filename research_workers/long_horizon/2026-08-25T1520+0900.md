## Newly covered fresh evidence

### 1. StagedWorkspace: version every parsed/native/review/submission view of persistent work
Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18050
Date: 2026-08-18
Mechanism: impose a workspace-state contract where parsed/searchable records and review diffs are explicitly bound to content hashes of native files. If native state changes, stale derived views are not silently treated as current.
Evidence: fixed-harness ablations on OfficeQA Pro and APEX-Agents. Dual parsed/native access has the highest point estimate for every tested model; vs the more limiting single view it improves OfficeQA Pass@1 by 8.3–12.1 points and APEX mean rubric score by 4.7–9.2 points. SW-AGENT reports 63.9% with Gemini 3.1 Pro on OfficeQA and 42.1 with GPT-5.4 Nano on APEX, compared with published same-model scores 29.3% and 25.5. A paired review-axis ablation on 57 editing tasks also favors visible diffs.
Judgment: VERY HIGH for O. This is concrete matched evidence for O's freshness/provenance direction: parsed observations, execution state, reviewed diff, and final artifact need explicit version bindings. A richer context kernel cannot compensate for reasoning over stale representations.

### 2. Outcome Monitors: recovery affordances can matter more than diagnostic explanation
Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19303
Date: 2026-08-19
Mechanism: mine/derive outcome contracts for tool results. If a result is syntactically valid but violates a semantic contract, preserve the observation and issue a non-binding receipt naming the violated property plus available public recovery tools.
Evidence: frozen prespecified injected-failure tests raise ToolMaze completion from 10.9% to 28.1% across four models/two provider families and replicate in a third. Tau-bench retail improves +14.0 and +12.0 points on two tiers. Controls show that removing the recovery-tool list eliminates the gain and restoring it restores the gain; diagnostic detail and receipt timing do not detectably matter. Out-of-vocabulary incident detection falls to 46%.
Judgment: VERY HIGH. When O detects a stale/invalid observation or failed action, a concrete list of viable recovery affordances may be more valuable than a longer explanation of the error. Context Kernel nodes should expose actions/alternatives, not merely status prose.

### 3. BENCH2ROBUST: classify failure semantics and choose retry vs switch vs stop
Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11977
Date: 2026-08-12
Mechanism: inject controlled transient, persistent, and silent tool failures into otherwise solvable tasks; train/evaluate policies that must retry the same route, switch to another route, or abstain when no viable path remains. Bayesian Tool Memory supplies environment-specific runtime recovery context; curriculum RL learns complementary recovery behavior.
Evidence: 7 models from 4 families across two multi-turn benchmark families. On held-out Retail, BTM improves robustness by up to +16.8 pp without retraining; BTM+RL reaches 40.8–45.5% under injected failures while preserving failure-free performance.
Judgment: VERY HIGH. O's generic "route around blockers" rule should be made more typed: transient infrastructure -> bounded retry; persistent route failure -> alternative path; exhausted trustworthy paths -> defer/escalate. Retrying should be a classified decision, not a reflex.

### 4. COTA: constructive runtime intervention can use a tiny comparison-only model instead of a second solver
Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21027
Date: 2026-08-21
Mechanism: actor proposes an action; sample K executable alternatives; a 0.5B comparator judges same-state pairs only, estimating whether enough alternatives dominate the proposal. If so, return the best alternative as non-binding advice and let the original actor replan. Comparator supervision comes from same-prefix counterfactual branches, isolating the branch action's downstream effect while keeping the continuation actor fixed.
Evidence: evaluated on WebShop, ALFWorld, and tau^3-Retail with Qwen3-8B, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, and DeepSeek-V4-Flash—nine actor/environment combinations. Primary paper reports improvement in all nine settings and strongest overall results among compared intervention baselines despite using Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct as comparator. Test operating points use K=4 alternatives, with intervention thresholds selected on held-out end-to-end validation rather than comparator accuracy.
Judgment: VERY HIGH architectural relevance and potential cost advantage. O may not need another Work-class model for continuous oversight; some action-quality gates could be delegated to a cheap narrow comparator trained/evaluated on same-prefix branches. Critical point: optimize the gate on end-to-end outcome, not critic accuracy alone.

## Cross-paper synthesis

- **Freshness is partly a workspace contract, not a prompting problem**: StagedWorkspace gives direct evidence that synchronized native/parsed/review views improve agent outcomes.
- **Error messages should be actionable**: Outcome Monitors isolate recovery-tool availability as the active ingredient, not verbose diagnosis.
- **Recovery decisions should be typed**: BENCH2ROBUST separates retry, switch, and abstain; O should distinguish blocker classes rather than loop on a generic "continue" policy.
- **Oversight need not duplicate the actor**: COTA shows a narrow comparison primitive can improve a much stronger actor. This supports cheaper continuous action gating than spawning another full solver.
- **Same-prefix counterfactual branches are an especially clean evaluation primitive**: they can train/measure intervention quality while holding prior context and continuation policy fixed.

## Candidate interventions for O

1. **Version-bound observation contract**: every parsed/extracted representation used in O decision context carries source artifact hash/revision; invalidate automatically when source changes.
2. **Recovery-affordance receipts**: on detected failure/staleness, surface a short typed failure plus currently legal alternatives, not a long diagnostic essay.
3. **Typed blocker policy**: classify transient vs persistent vs silent/uncertain failure and precommit retry/switch/defer behavior with bounded retries.
4. **Cheap comparator gate**: on high-consequence actions, generate a few plausible alternatives and use the smallest adequate comparator to decide whether the proposed action is dominated; actor retains final replanning.
5. **Counterfactual branch dataset**: use identical prefix/environment checkpoints to compare candidate actions and learn intervention rules from downstream outcomes.

## Evidence-scope cautions
- StagedWorkspace is knowledge-work/file-centric; hash-bound derived-view freshness generalizes structurally, but exact gains do not.
- Outcome Monitor detection degrades outside its contract vocabulary; a monitor is only as broad as its mined/formal contracts.
- BENCH2ROBUST still has low absolute completion under injected failures, so typed recovery is helpful but far from solving robustness.
- COTA requires candidate generation and same-prefix branch supervision; its online cost must be compared against the expected saved failures in O.
